# BigBasket Category Performance — Data Story

## 1. The Big Picture

The analysis starts with a total Delivered revenue of **₹88,282 from 434 orders** across six categories.

At first glance, the performance looks fairly balanced — **3 categories are above their targets and 3 are below target**. However, looking a little deeper shows that some categories are performing much better than others.

---

## 2. The Categories Leading the Way

The strongest performance comes from **Household Essentials**.

- Revenue: **₹21,715**
- Target: **₹17,000**
- Above target by: **₹4,715**
- Percentage above target: **27.74%**

This is the highest-revenue category in the analysis and has the largest positive variance against its target.

**Bakery** is another strong performer.

- Revenue: **₹15,410**
- Target: **₹12,000**
- Above target by: **₹3,410**
- Percentage above target: **28.42%**

Interestingly, Bakery has the **highest percentage improvement over its target**, even though its total revenue is lower than Household Essentials.

Finally, **Personal Care** is slightly ahead of its target.

- Revenue: **₹16,382**
- Target: **₹15,500**
- Above target by: **₹882**
- Percentage above target: **5.69%**

Together, these three categories show that half of the categories are currently meeting or exceeding expectations.

---

## 3. Where the Story Changes

The other half of the categories are falling short of their targets.

**Dairy & Eggs** is the closest to recovering its target.

- Revenue: **₹14,090**
- Target: **₹16,500**
- Shortfall: **₹2,410**
- Percentage below target: **14.61%**
- Status: **Below Target - Watch**

This category is worth watching because it is below target, but its shortfall is smaller than the critical categories.

The bigger concern comes from **Fruits & Vegetables**.

- Revenue: **₹9,790**
- Target: **₹12,000**
- Shortfall: **₹2,210**
- Percentage below target: **18.42%**
- Status: **Below Target - Critical**

It also has the **lowest revenue among all six categories**.

**Snacks & Beverages** faces a similar problem.

- Revenue: **₹10,895**
- Target: **₹13,000**
- Shortfall: **₹2,105**
- Percentage below target: **16.19%**
- Status: **Below Target - Critical**

Both Fruits & Vegetables and Snacks & Beverages are therefore the categories that need the most attention.

---

## 4. What the Numbers Suggest

There is a clear split in the category performance:

- **Household Essentials** and **Bakery** are the strongest performers.
- **Personal Care** is performing slightly above target.
- **Dairy & Eggs** is below target but relatively close to its goal.
- **Fruits & Vegetables** and **Snacks & Beverages** are the two critical areas.

The two critical categories have a combined shortfall of **₹4,315**.

On the other hand, Household Essentials and Bakery together are **₹8,125 above their combined targets**.

This creates a clear opportunity: maintain the momentum in the stronger categories while working to close the gap in the weaker ones.

---

## 5. Recommendations

### Recommendation 1 — Focus on the Two Critical Categories

The first priority should be **Fruits & Vegetables and Snacks & Beverages**.

Both are classified as **Below Target - Critical**, with a combined shortfall of **₹4,315**. Reducing this gap would directly improve overall category performance.

### Recommendation 2 — Protect the Strong Performers

The strong performance of **Household Essentials and Bakery** should be maintained.

They are currently **₹8,125 above their combined targets**, with Household Essentials contributing ₹4,715 and Bakery contributing ₹3,410 of that surplus.

---

## 6. Final Takeaway

The overall picture is positive, but there is a noticeable gap between the strongest and weakest categories.

**Household Essentials and Bakery are driving the strongest performance, while Fruits & Vegetables and Snacks & Beverages are pulling category performance below target.**

The next focus should therefore be simple: **protect what is already working and reduce the shortfall in the two critical categories.**
